## 🚀 Project Overview

**Computer Store Sales Dashboard — 2025** is an interactive **Microsoft Excel Data Analytics project** built to analyze and visualize computer store sales performance across products, brands, cities, and months.

The project transforms raw transactional sales data into a structured analytical solution using **Excel Tables, Pivot Tables, Pivot-based analysis, charts, filters, and an interactive dashboard**.

The primary objective is to turn raw sales records into **clear business insights that can support performance monitoring and decision-making**.

> **From Raw Sales Data → Analysis → Visualization → Business Insights**

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The dashboard is designed to answer important business questions such as:

* 💰 How much total revenue was generated?
* 📈 How did sales perform throughout the year?
* 🏆 Which products generated the highest revenue?
* 🏷️ Which brands performed best?
* 🌆 Which cities generated the most sales?
* 📦 Which products had the highest demand?
* 📊 How does product performance vary across different cities?
* 📅 Which months generated the highest and lowest sales?
* 🔎 Where are the strongest sales opportunities?

# 💡 Key Business Insights

Based on the underlying analysis:

### 🥇 Product Performance

**Laptop** is the highest-revenue product with **₹21.31 Lakh** in sales.

### 🥈 Brand Performance

**Samsung** leads brand performance with **₹18.64 Lakh** in sales.

### 🥉 Geographic Performance

**Bengaluru** generates the highest city-level revenue at **₹16.35 Lakh**.

### 📅 Monthly Performance

**December** is the strongest month with **₹13.51 Lakh** in sales.

### 📦 Demand

**Mouse** has the highest recorded quantity sold with **144 units**, followed by Keyboard and Headphones.

### 🔎 Important Analytical Insight

Revenue leadership and quantity leadership are not necessarily the same.

This highlights why sales analysis should consider both:

**Revenue + Volume**

rather than relying on a single metric.
